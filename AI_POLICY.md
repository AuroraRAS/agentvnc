AgentVNC AI policy
==================

AgentVNC trusts and actively welcomes contributions generated with the
assistance of AI, including code, tests, documentation, and
translations, as long as they fulfill the project's normal standards for
quality.

AI-assisted contributions are subject only to the following requirements
for transparency and human oversight:

Transparency
------------

Commits containing AI-assisted or AI-generated content must be marked
with an "Assisted-by:" tag for transparency.

Human involvement and oversight
-------------------------------

All contributions must be submitted by a human who has reviewed them,
understands them, and is able to answer questions about them. Bug
reports, pull requests, and comments written with AI assistance should
likewise be reviewed and verified by the contributor before posting.
